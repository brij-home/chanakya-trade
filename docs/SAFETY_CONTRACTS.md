# Verified market safety contracts

Effective review date: 1 November 2026. These contracts apply to human and AI changes equally, regardless of model/provider. References describe their publication and effective dates separately.

- Execution mode and data provenance are independent. PAPER/SIMULATE permits simulated execution, never invented quotes, option OI/volume or order-book quantities for actionable real-market alerts. Explicit mock DEMO scenarios are isolated. Production Telegram gates must reject explicit synthetic/test evidence even if other metadata claims LIVE.
- Expiry weekdays come from market.rule_schedule and exchange holiday adjustment/listed contracts. NSE has Tuesday expiry and BSE Thursday from September 2025. A single weekly index rule does not imply Thursday/Friday expiry. Historical tests must retain their historical effective rules.
- Current NSE sessions distinguish CAS-eligible cash stocks, other cash stocks, and equity derivatives. The October 15 announcement's December 7, 2026 / February 8, 2027 changes must not be activated early. See the dated session functions and the review's remaining integration work.
- Securities futures/options-sale STT changes on April 1, 2026 from 0.02%/0.10% to 0.05%/0.15%. Historical backtests supply the historical date. Exercise tax uses intrinsic value. Do not change current fixtures to make an old rate pass.
- Zero DTE is expiry day, never missing metadata. A risk budget may yield zero lots. No one-lot floor may erase a mandatory reduction.
- Missing stop/target evidence remains unset in a ticket. Percentage defaults do not constitute a structural blueprint.
- PAPER emergency handling cannot touch the configured real broker. Submission/cancellation requests are not verified fills/flat positions. Unknowns require reconciliation.
- Platform live order placement is additionally disabled unless the owner sets `LIVE_EXECUTION_QUALIFIED=1` after the account-scoped execution ledger, unknown-attempt recovery, fill-driven protection and broker acceptance criteria are complete. The review has not completed those criteria; do not set this flag as a workaround. A rejected live safety gate leaves the intent `CONFIRMED`; it must not be stranded as `SUBMITTING` when no broker call was made.
- Live multi-order baskets, freeze-limit slices, and automatic protective GTT entries remain blocked even behind the qualification gate until child order IDs, partial fills, protection acknowledgements, and restart recovery are durable and tested. Broker basket placement is not portfolio-level atomicity. SIMULATE retains sequential PaperBroker behavior.
- Provider-specific Fyers routes for GTT/smart/multileg placement, position bracket/conversion changes, and untracked GTT cancellation are not order-authority alternatives. They return a conflict until implemented through the OMS. Fyers panic-exit must use the audited central emergency coordinator; pending broker acknowledgement is not proof of a flat account.
- Money writes spanning multiple statements use an explicit writer transaction and rollback on failure; pooled acquisition alone is not a transaction. Audit appends serialize the read-head/write-record sequence.
- A centered retrospective swing is not a causal signal. Strategies must pass prefix invariance and reachable bullish/bearish controls.
- Detector history below its configured minimum is UNCALIBRATED: show zero empirical edge statistics and apply no alpha bonus. A one-sided winning sample also remains UNCALIBRATED because it cannot estimate downside; a sufficiently large one-sided losing sample may degrade or halt the detector based on observed negative outcomes. Never seed win-rate, payoff or Kelly metrics with assumed values.
- Alert lifecycle outcomes are forward-price observations, not verified broker fills or account P&L. A terminal forward-path record requires an explicit current exit quote plus immutable entry and initial-stop anchors. Skip untriggered setups, stale quote fallbacks, partial-scale outcomes, runners, and missing anchors until weighted fills are durable. These path observations may conservatively degrade detectors, but cannot produce a `HEALTHY` status, positive alpha bonus or Kelly allocation. Historical records without provenance remain audit-only.
- Pattern-learning milestones are alert events, not account wins. Performance win rate and realized R remain `null` until sourced from an independently reconciled fill ledger. Remove invented seed cases; preserve missing features/performance values as unavailable; exclude unverified fingerprints from similarity matching. Milestone observations do not recalibrate trade-performance factor weights.
- A caller-supplied `VERIFIED_BROKER_FILL` label is insufficient evidence and is rejected by the current detector writer. Persisted rows with that label are also excluded pending an account-scoped reconciled fill-ledger integration. `TEST` calibration records are admitted only during pytest and cannot affect a normal application run.
- Historical fingerprints must use information available before the event. Current option chains, sector rankings and fundamental caches cannot describe a historical setup; keep those features unavailable until a timestamped as-of source exists. A large past move does not establish a confidence score. Neutral/unknown or multi-leg payoffs cannot be evaluated as a single long price path.
- Resting bid/ask quantities are not traded volume. A TBT book cannot supply session OHLC, OI, circuit limits or exchange session VWAP from its last price or rolling tick average. Keep unavailable fields `null` with field status. TBT depth requires a LIVE source and a finite, nonfuture local packet age within the TBT freshness budget; never extend it through a five-minute REST cache. SDK capability checks must import the actual transport submodule, not only the top-level package.
- Regime classification may consume only an explicit caller VIX value or a provider-identified LIVE India VIX quote with an aware, finite, nonfuture receipt timestamp no older than 30 seconds. Missing/stale/future/unverified VIX yields `UNAVAILABLE`; never replace it with a neutral numeric baseline or use a private cache without age checks. Regime-governed new alerts fail closed while unavailable.
- RVOL-governed breakouts require measured RVOL; no test-runner substitute is allowed. `OPTIONS_MOMENTUM` is a separate contract-flow detector: its gate requires positive contract volume and OI evidence, not an invented underlying RVOL. Strict `FNO_ONLY` ingestion accepts only classified index/stock F&O alerts; cash-equity context must not become a separate cash alert desk.
- ATM subscription grids must be selected from active canonical master rows using a fresh, LIVE underlying quote. Require matching listed CE/PE contracts, actual expiry/venue/lot/tick/token values, and fail closed when the master or quote is unavailable. Never generate plausible symbols from a guessed spot, strike step or weekday calendar.
- Implied-volatility rank must be computed from historical option-IV observations. Underlying realized-volatility rank is a separate metric and must never be exposed as IV rank or used to claim IV richness, IV crush, or premium edge. Missing or flat IV history is `UNAVAILABLE`, never a synthetic neutral 50. Event option structures remain research-only, without actionable risk/reward, until source-qualified option premiums, IV history, event timing and leg-level payoff inputs are present; research-only metadata cannot increase alert conviction.
- Expiry classification and physical-settlement windows use the effective-dated market schedule, exchange holiday adjustment, and listed contract expiry when available. Never use stale Thursday assumptions or a calendar month-end guess. A zero-DTE intraday exit policy requires explicit listed expiry date or explicit sourced DTE=0 metadata; weekday-only guesses cannot trigger accelerated theta exits.
- All expiry-sensitive detector scoring (including index penny-option/OTM penalties) requires the active chain's explicit expiry and the shared 0DTE resolver. A weekday, underlying name, or time-of-day alone cannot activate a 0DTE rule.
- Max Pain is descriptive positioning context only. It cannot add bullish/bearish conviction, imply pinning/mean reversion, or forecast a target without independently validated out-of-sample evidence. Do not restore weekday-derived expiry gravity.
- Options backtests resolve listed expiries per underlying and request only the matching canonical broker symbol. They must not reuse NIFTY weeklies for stock/monthly-only contracts or mix weekly/monthly history under one expiry. Daily-close and Black–Scholes backtests are research-only estimates without intraday spread, latency, queue, partial-fill or impact simulation; do not present their returns as executable alpha.
- FYERS Prime API limits and features changed effective 16 October 2026. The owner has confirmed an active Prime subscription; keep the selected plan explicit and fail on unknown plan names rather than silently assuming Prime. The expired F&O adapter now calls FYERS' dedicated endpoint and parses optional OI/Greeks by named response columns. Nulls, absent columns, non-option contracts, unavailable ranges and endpoint errors remain explicitly unavailable. This API supports expired contracts only; it does not by itself create a current, comparable constant-tenor IV history. It must not be used to claim IV rank, current IV richness, or actionable signal conviction until that time-series construction, as-of controls and held-out validation exist. Sources: FYERS [rate-limit notice](https://fyers.in/notice-board/changes-to-fyers-api-rate-limits-and-usage) and [expired F&O API](https://fyers.in/api-docs/v3/api/market-data/expired-fo-data/get-expired-fo-data).
- FYERS access tokens expire daily at 06:30 IST, regardless of issuance time. The adapter's token freshness boundary and regression tests must track this provider-documented cutoff; do not use the obsolete 06:00 assumption or a 12-hour TTL. Source: FYERS [authentication flow](https://fyers.in/api-docs/v3/api/authentication/individual-app/authentication-flow), last updated 14 October 2026.
- Exchange quantity-freeze limits are effective-dated contract data, not stable constants. `market.symbol_master.get_quantity_freeze_info()` resolves `qtyFreeze` from a fresh, non-degraded published F&O master and refuses missing, inactive, stale or ambiguous data. Order planning must propagate that failure; never add static per-underlying values or lot-multiple heuristics. Slices must be whole canonical lots and cannot exceed the published quantity. Live freeze-limit slicing remains blocked pending durable child-order tracking and recovery. See the [NSE contract information page](https://www.nseindia.com/static/products-services/equity-derivatives-contract-information), updated 26 October 2026.

## Change procedure

### Discovery scope additions, 1 November 2026

- `config.discovery_policy` owns the validated FNO_ONLY/FNO_PRIORITY/BALANCED profiles. FNO_ONLY is the default for new deployments and filters new discovery before feed/AI work; it is independent of PAPER/DEMO/LIVE execution. Never bypass this scope because CHANAKYA_TESTING is set. General multi-asset tests explicitly select BALANCED, while production-scope tests exercise FNO_ONLY.
- Discovery filtering must not decorate exit, target/trailing, guardian or reconciliation operations. Crypto transport may start for explicit existing-exposure monitoring even while new crypto discovery is excluded. Unknown-purpose persisted subscriptions must not be deleted merely to improve focus statistics; expose them in diagnostics until their purpose can be reconciled.
- Scanner cache keys must incorporate strict discovery scope so a previous broad result cannot leak across a profile switch. New subscription seeds and ticker polling obey the context policy. Futures/constituent context is not permission to publish a cash setup.
- TBT must retain all exposure registrations and allocate the provider's available slots to exposure before discovery. Do not pop the oldest position at a four-contract limit. Report uncovered subscriptions when capacity is exceeded; successful subscription is not evidence of fresh usable depth or verified protective execution.

1. Trace the real caller and downstream Telegram/UI/accounting consequences before editing.
2. Cite an exchange/regulator/broker primary source with publication and effective date when changing a market rule.
3. Add or update behavior tests at the real boundary, including negative scenarios and BULL/BEAR/NEUTRAL impact where applicable.
4. Run tests/test_verified_market_safety.py and affected integration suites. Existing tests that encode stale rules must be corrected with independent evidence, not preserved blindly.
5. Submit the source and tests together for owner review. Do not weaken/remove safety checks or alter fixtures simply to obtain a green build.
6. Never enable live-trading flags, submit orders, repair historical production alerts at startup, or send Telegram messages as part of a test.
